MESSAGE: That hunger 2–3 hours after a meal can be a blood-sugar dip, not a willpower problem.

WHY: Our audience blames themselves for the 4 pm snack attack. This large glucose-monitor study links the dip after a sugar spike to more hunger and more eating later — a body signal, not a character flaw. It reframes guilt into curiosity ('what did I eat at lunch?') without promising weight loss. We flag that it is an association, the effect is small, and 97% of participants were white.
