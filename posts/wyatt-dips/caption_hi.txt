Proper lunch ke baad bhi 4 baje zor ki bhookh? 1070 adults par glucose monitor lagakar ek study ne dekha ki khaane ke 2–3 ghante baad blood sugar ka GIRNA (dip) — spike se zyada — bhookh aur baad mein khaane ko predict karta hai. Sabse bade dip waale log agle 24 ghante mein lagbhag 312 kcal zyada khaaye.

Study mein sabse bade dip us test meal ke baad aaye jisme sugar spike sabse zyada tha (glucose drink). Dhyan rahe: ye association hai, effect chhota hai, khaana self-reported tha, aur 97% log white the — Indian khaana test nahi hua. Bhookh ke kai kaaran hain; ye unme se ek hai.

Source: Wyatt et al., Nature Metabolism 2021 (doi:10.1038/s42255-021-00383-x)
