Lagta hai BMI 'theek-thaak' hai toh sab theek? India ki revised obesity definition zyada dhyan se dekhti hai. Paanch round ke Delphi consensus mein Indian experts ne Asian Indians ke liye BMI 23 kg/m² se upar ko starting point rakha — aur kamar bhi jodi (purush 90 cm+, mahila 80 cm+, ya kamar ÷ height 0.5 se upar).

Obesity ko stages mein baanta: Stage 1 (extra fat, organs ya daily life pe asar nahi — lifestyle aksar kaafi) aur Stage 2 (saath mein daily-life dikkat jaise saans phoolna ya jodon ka dard, ya related bimari). Dhyan rahe: ye expert consensus hai, naya trial data nahi.

Source: Misra et al., Diabetes Metab Syndr 2025 (doi:10.1016/j.dsx.2024.102989)
