Think a 'normal-looking' BMI means you're fine? India's revised obesity definition looks closer. In a five-round Delphi consensus, Indian experts set BMI above 23 kg/m² as the starting point for Asian Indians — and added the waist (90 cm+ men, 80 cm+ women, or waist-to-height above 0.5).

They also split obesity into stages: Stage 1 (extra fat, no effect on organs or daily life — lifestyle is usually enough) and Stage 2 (plus daily-life limits like breathlessness or leg-joint pain, or a related disease). Note: this is expert consensus, not new trial data.

Source: Misra et al., Diabetes Metab Syndr 2025 (doi:10.1016/j.dsx.2024.102989)
