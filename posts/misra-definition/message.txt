MESSAGE: India's new obesity definition looks beyond the scale: BMI above 23, your waist, and whether daily life or health is affected.

WHY: Our audience either thinks 'BMI 25 is fine' (Western cut-offs) or feels labelled by one number. The new Indian expert definition is empowering: Indian-specific cut-offs, a waist check anyone can do at home, and a staging that separates 'extra fat, no harm yet' (Stage 1, where lifestyle is usually enough) from 'it's affecting my body' (Stage 2, see a doctor). It moves the conversation from weight to health without promoting any drug.
