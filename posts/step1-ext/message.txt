MESSAGE: Weight coming back after stopping treatment is biology, not failure — so plan for maintenance from day one.

WHY: Regain is the #1 fear in our GLP-1 user research ('agar band kiya toh sab wapas?'). This trial answers it honestly: yes, most of it came back when BOTH the medicine and lifestyle support stopped — but not all, and the body's pushback is biological, not a willpower failure. That replaces shame with a plan and points to a doctor-led maintenance conversation, without promoting any drug (CDSCO March 2026).
