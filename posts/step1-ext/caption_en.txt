"If I stop, will it all come back?" It's the question we hear most. In the STEP 1 extension, adults stopped a weekly weight-loss injection AND their lifestyle support at week 68. A year later, about two-thirds of the lost weight had returned — but on average they were still 5.6% below where they started, and 48.2% kept off at least 5%.

Regain isn't a willpower failure; after weight loss the body pushes back. So any plan needs an exit plan. Note: most participants were White adults in 5 high-income countries, and several authors work for the manufacturer.

Source: Wilding et al., Diabetes Obes Metab 2022 (doi:10.1111/dom.14725)
