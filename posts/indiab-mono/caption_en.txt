"My BMI is normal, I'm fine" — maybe not. ICMR's national INDIAB study found 43.3% of Indian adults had a BMI under 25 but two or more metabolic risk factors (big waist, high BP, sugar, triglycerides or low HDL). Only 26.6% were normal-BMI AND metabolically healthy. This group had about 6.9 times the odds of type 2 diabetes vs healthy normal-weight adults.

The fix starts simple: a measuring tape, a BP check, a fasting sugar test. Note: it's a one-time survey, so it can't prove cause and effect.

Source: Deepa et al., Indian J Med Res 2025 (doi:10.25259/IJMR_328_2025)
