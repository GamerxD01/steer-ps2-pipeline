MESSAGE: In India, a 'normal' BMI doesn't mean metabolically healthy — check your waist, BP and sugar, not just the scale.

WHY: Our research found 'I'm not fat, so this isn't for me' and 'BMI says normal' as common blind spots. This national ICMR study shows the biggest group of Indian adults is normal-BMI but metabolically unhealthy, with higher diabetes odds. It turns a scary number into one simple action (measure waist, check BP/sugar) and positions steer as the place for a doctor-led metabolic check — without promoting any medicine.
