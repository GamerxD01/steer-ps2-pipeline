"Mera BMI normal hai, sab theek hai" — shayad nahi. ICMR ki national INDIAB study mein 43.3% Indian adults ka BMI 25 se kam tha, par 2 ya zyada metabolic risk factors the (badi kamar, high BP, sugar, triglycerides ya kam HDL). Sirf 26.6% log normal BMI AUR metabolically healthy the. Is group mein type 2 diabetes ke odds lagbhag 6.9 guna the.

Shuruaat simple hai: ek measuring tape, BP check, fasting sugar test. Dhyan rahe: ye ek-baar ka survey hai, cause-effect prove nahi karta.

Source: Deepa et al., Indian J Med Res 2025 (doi:10.25259/IJMR_328_2025)
